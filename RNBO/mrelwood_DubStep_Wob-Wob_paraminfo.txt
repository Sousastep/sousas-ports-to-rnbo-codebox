DubStep Wob-Wob — mrelwood plugins
Page: https://mrelwood5.wixsite.com/plugins
(Wob-Wob has no individual page on the site; it is listed under Gain in
the mrelwood plugin list and ships inside the free mrelwood_PluginPack.)

What it is: the famous dubstep wobble - a volume (gain) automator driven
by a pattern in beats. It is NOT a filter: it modulates simple gain, so you
put it BEFORE your own filter to get the classic wub. The pattern is
programmed into 56 step buttons driven by the beat inlet. IMPORTANT for
the RNBO port: in3 is the CONTINUOUS BEAT POSITION in quarter notes (a
0..N ramp you supply), and all pattern buttons default OFF, so it passes
minimum gain until something is programmed and the beat is connected
(README note).

Parameters (original names):

- slider1 (Freq Min) (Hz) 20..1000, default 20
  The gain output stays at its MINIMUM gain while the pattern says so.
  Unlike the real candidates above this plugin is volume-only, so this
  slider instead sets where the modulation viewport starts on the
  audio - think "floor" of the wobble. 200-500 Hz is a musical
  sawage range, 20 is an on/off feel.

- slider2 (Freq Max) (Hz) 100..20000, default 2000
  Where the modulation tops out - the "ceiling" of the wobble shape.
  Together with slider1 this shapes the face of the wobble ramp; with
  a wide gap the range is dramatic, with a narrow gap it becomes an
  on/off stutter.

- slider3 (Offset) (beats) -4..+4, default 0
  Slide the whole pattern left or right in beats relative to the beat
  position. Positive = shift the pattern later. It is the knob you
  turn to synchronize the wobble accents with other tracks.

- slider4 (Steepness) 3..15, default 5
  How sharply the wobble steps change: higher = snappier, more
  staircase-like transitions (read "buttery"). Lower values (3-5)
  keep a smooth, liquid wobble; above 8 you get hard steps
  ("steppiness lasts forever", use with care).

- sliderstep1..56 (Pattern buttons) 0/1, default all 0
  The wobble pattern itself (56 slots). Slots 1-32 are spaced at 1/32 of
  4 beats; slots 33-56 use the source's subsequent 1/24 spacing
  (README notes). 0 = step off, 1 = step on. Use e.g.
  'sliderstep1 1' to hear the basic wobble at minimum, then add
  every 4th step for a classic "1-1-7" pattern.

Notes for the RNBO port: pattern default-off means the module sits at
MINIMUM gain until a beat position is provided and at least one step is
turned ON - it does not generate its own tempo clock, REAPER-style
pattern serialization is not ported (README note). You are expected to
supply the beat ramp yourself (Max LE temporal clock, MIDI, or a ramp).
