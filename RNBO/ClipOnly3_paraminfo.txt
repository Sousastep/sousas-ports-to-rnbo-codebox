ClipOnly3 — Airwindows (Chris Johnson)
Page: https://www.airwindows.com/cliponly3/

What it is: a transparent "safety brickwall". It only acts on audio that
would actually clip: below ±0.90850431 it changes nothing at all, so it is
safe to leave on any channel or buss. This RNBO port has NO parameters - the
threshold, soft-clip shape and slew control are fixed in the algorithm.

No parameters:

- (none)
  Internal behavior, for reference:
  * soft-clip threshold ±0.9085097 (about -0.84 dBFS), hard overshoot
    reconstruction to keep clipped tops from sounding "digital"
  * fixes clipped tops in a very narrow band, not the whole waveform
  * behaves like a nonlinear lowpass control (based on slew) to tame
    overs and digital harshness without dulling the material
  * consumes a small amount of CPU; designed never to be bypassed, you
    just leave it in the chain

Musical notes: use it where you would otherwise reach for a limiter but
don't want one - before a saturator or clipper, or on a buss you keep
relatively unprocessed. It prints a slightly warmer "slide" over any
overshoots rather than a hard-topped square.

Source quirks (see RNBO/README.md): the port initializes lastDryL/R to 0
(the source leaves them undefined); behavior otherwise matches the source.
