ZAcidLowpass — Airwindows (Chris Johnson)
Page: https://www.airwindows.com/zacidlowpass/

What it is: the "acid" member of the Z filter family: a resonant lowpass
built from Bezier undersampling plus an Emu e6400-style output clipping
stage, plus an unstable "meltdown" control that feeds the raw audio into
the filter's control voltage path. It is a filter-meets-ring-modulator
experiment; the resonance is nasty in a good way. This RNBO port uses the
original normalized 0..1 values and keeps the fixed 15.5 kHz op-amp HP/LP
filters - so use sample rates ABOVE 31 kHz (README note).

Parameters:

- p_cutoff (Cutoff) 0..1, default 0.5
  Filter frequency, but with a cubic taper: internally pow(p, 3) scaled to
  sample rate, so most of the musical range (about 100 Hz - 15 kHz for
  typical SR) lives in the top half; the bottom third is extremely low.
  A resonant-sounding lowpass through the Bezier curve, not a biquad.

- p_over (Over) 0..1, default 0.0
  "Over" behavior: together with Under it makes a sort of crossover. With
  Over only, tones above the cutoff are passed and boosted  (a peppy
  YAHTC-like resonant filter that pushes highs). With Meltdown combined it
  becomes the MOST aggressive: with Over at 1 and Meltdown at 0 you get
  the famous "crazy overtones and harsh noise" setting. Linear 0..1.

- p_under (Under) 0..1, default 1.0
  The complementary half of the "crossover": Under is the passive lowpass
  accent. Over + Under both at 1 near the midpoint approximates the plain
  resonant lowpass; Over alone is a distinctly gritty highpassed tone.

- p_meltdwn (Meltdown) 0..1, default 0.0
  Feeds the raw (pre-filter) audio directly into the control voltage path
  instead of the averaged/darkened one: more aliasing, chaos, and
  ring-mod-flavored artifacts. Linear; moderate values already sound
  "broken".

- p_drive (Drive) 0..1, default 0.1
  Input trim into the Z-style distortion stage. Internally a fourth-power
  mapping: (p*10)^4, from about x1 at 0.1 up to x10000 (+80 dB!) at 1.0 -
  a very steep taper with enormous range, designed for abuse. Default
  0.1 is roughly unity.

- p_output (Output) 0..1, default 1.0
  Output pad before the fixed 15.5 kHz stage. Linear trim.

Musical notes: works as a real acid box: Cutoff + Resonance-taste, then
push Drive and Meltdown for industrial textures; or with Over for
highpassed stabs. Because of the fixed 15.5 kHz filter, do NOT use
low sample rates (README note above).
